# The Reading Room

This chapter is a test fixture for the reader. It is not part of any book by the author. Its only purpose is to check that pages turn, that narration plays, and that the highlighted sentence follows the voice.

Imagine a quiet room at the end of a long corridor. The window faces east, and the morning light falls across a wooden table. On the table there is a single book, open at the first page. The paper is the colour of warm ivory, and the letters are dark and clear.

A reader enters the room and sits down. She turns the first page slowly. The sound of the paper is soft, like a breath. Outside, a bird calls once and then is silent. The reader begins to read, and the room seems to grow larger around her.

Every sentence in this test is short and simple. That makes it easy to see when the highlight moves. When the narrator reaches the end of a sentence, the highlight should move to the next one. When the narration reaches the bottom of a page, the page should turn by itself.

## A Second Section

Now the light has moved across the table. The reader has finished several pages. She pauses to look out of the window, and then returns to the book. Nothing has been lost. The book remembers her place, and so does the reader.

If you pause the narration, the highlight should stay where it is. If you press play again, it should continue from the same sentence. If you switch from the book view to the listening view, you should arrive at the very same place in the text.

You can also change the size of the letters. When the text grows larger, there are more pages, but the narrated sentence should still be found on the correct page. This is the purpose of measuring each sentence after the layout is complete.

The afternoon arrives. The shadows in the room grow longer and softer. The reader closes her eyes for a moment and listens. The voice continues, steady and calm, and the words appear one after another on the page.

At last the chapter comes to an end. The reader marks her place and stands up. Tomorrow she will return to this room, and the book will open exactly where she left it.
